## Product & Company
**What is your product or service?**
A brief description of what it does, who it's for, and the core problem it solves. LogAlert Al is a lightweight, Al-powered log monitoring tool designed for engineering teams. It automatically scans application logs in real-time to detect, explain, and suggest fixes for software bugs before users notice them.

**What type of company are you?**
SaaS

**What pain points does your product solve?**
Alert Fatigue: Developers get flooded with thousands of meaningless warning notifications every day, causing them to miss actual, critical system crashes. Slow Debugging Time: When a system breaks, engineers waste hours digging through raw text logs trying to figure out exactly what line of code caused the error.
