## Product & Company
**What is your product or service?**
ClinicPulse is an Al-powered healthcare receptionist designed for medical and dental clinics. It automatically handles 24/7 patient booking, instant insurance verification, and automated post-appointment follow-up calls. It integrates directly with standard Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Practice Management systems to eliminate administrative overhead and reduce phone drop-off rates.

**What type of company are you?**
SaaS

**What pain points does your product solve?**
High Call Abandonment: Clinics lose up to 30% of potential new patient bookings because front-desk staff are busy or calls come in after-hours. Administrative Burnout: Receptionists spend hours on repetitive tasks like manual insurance verification and chasing patients for follow-up appointments.
