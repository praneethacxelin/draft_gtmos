# Discovery Brief: Freshdesk
Generated at: 2026-06-24 14:37:26

## Product & Company
**What is your product or service?**
Freshdesk is a cloud-based customer support software that helps businesses manage customer tickets across email, phone, chat, social media, and messaging. It provides a unified helpdesk with AI-powered automations, self-service portals, and SLA management for mid-market and enterprise support teams.

**What type of company are you?**
SaaS

**What pain points does your product solve?**
Support teams drown in ticket volume with no way to prioritize. Agents waste time switching between email, chat, and phone tools. Managers have zero visibility into SLA compliance. Customer satisfaction drops because of slow first-response times and no self-service options.
